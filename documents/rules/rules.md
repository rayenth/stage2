#### **1. [[Coverage Provisions]]**

These clauses define what the insurer will pay for. They outline the specific perils or events that are covered and the services or items that will be paid for.

- **Example Rule:** "The first 10 visits to the doctor are covered."
    
- **How to Write It:** This rule is a **limitation** on coverage. When drafting, you must be clear and specific.
    
    - **Clarity is Key:** State the number of visits, what constitutes a "visit," and any exclusions (e.g., "up to 10 visits per calendar year," "does not include specialist consultations").
        
    - **Drafting Example:** "The Company agrees to pay for the first ten (10) medically necessary doctor's office visits per Insured per Policy Year. Any subsequent visits will be subject to a 50% co-payment by the Insured."
        

#### **2. [[Limitations and Exclusions]]**

These are rules that specifically limit or exclude coverage. They define what the policy **does not** cover.

- **Example Rule:** "Pre-existing conditions are not covered."
    
- **How to Write It:** Exclusions must be clearly and conspicuously stated to be legally enforceable.
    
    - **Specific Language:** Use words like "excluding," "except for," or "does not apply to."
        
    - **Drafting Example:** "This policy does not cover any medical condition for which the Insured received diagnosis, advice, or treatment during the six (6) months prior to the Policy's effective date."
        

#### **3. [[Deductibles and Co-payments]]**

These are rules related to the insured's share of the cost. A **deductible** is the amount the insured must pay out-of-pocket before the insurer pays anything. A **co-payment** is a fixed amount the insured pays for a covered service after the deductible has been met.

- **Example Rule:** "A $25 co-payment is required for each doctor's visit."
    
- **How to Write It:** Clearly state the amount, the service it applies to, and when it is payable.
    
    - **Drafting Example:** "A co-payment of twenty-five dollars ($25) shall be payable by the Insured to the healthcare provider for each office visit."
        

#### **4. [[Conditions and Duties of the Insured]]**

These rules outline the responsibilities of the policyholder. Failure to comply can result in the denial of a claim.

- **Example Rule:** "The Insured must report all claims within 30 days of the loss."
    
- **How to Write It:** Use clear, mandatory language like "must" or "shall."
    
    - **Drafting Example:** "The Insured shall provide written notice of a claim to the Company within thirty (30) days following the date of the loss."
